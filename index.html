How to play Pen Fight

How to play Pen Fight

  1. 1. Pull back

    Press your pen and drag opposite the direction you want to shoot. A shorter pull gives a gentler flick.

  2. 2. Release

    Let go to hit an opponent. Aim for an open route to the nearest edge, then wait for the pens to settle.

  3. 3. Stay on

    Knock the other pen off without sending your own over the edge. Save space for your rebound.

Pen Fight FAQ

Free play, controls, friend rooms, mobile play and cosmetic skins.

01Can I play Pen Fight online for free?+

Yes. Pen Fight: Desk Legends is free to play in your browser on PenFight.org. No game download is required; choose guest play to get started.

02How do I play Pen Fight?+

Press your pen, pull back opposite the direction you want to shoot, then release. Knock the opponent off the desk while keeping your pen on it. Start with a short pull and wait for the pieces to stop.

03Can I play Pen Fight with friends?+

Yes. Open Multiplayer in the game, create a room and share its invitation link or room code. Rooms have up to four seats for free-for-all, 2v2 or cooperative Raid.

04Does Pen Fight work on mobile?+

Desktop is recommended. Phone and tablet controls are still being tested. If you try mobile, use landscape orientation and a current browser; touch and fullscreen behavior can vary by device.

05Do pen skins change gameplay?+

Pen skins change appearance and visual effects, not mass or shot strength. Classroom backgrounds are also cosmetic. Desk materials are separate: wood and plastic have different friction.
