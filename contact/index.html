Support desk

Contact

Email us

Send messages to mooyuking@gmail.com. Please use a clear subject line such as “Pen Fight technical issue,” “Content correction,” “Privacy request,” or “Rights request.”

Technical support

Include your device type, operating system, browser and version, the page you opened, and the steps that caused the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. A screenshot can help when the canvas is blank or touch coordinates appear offset.

Corrections and feedback

We welcome specific corrections to control instructions, compatibility notes, credits, and factual descriptions. Tell us which paragraph or page should change and, where possible, include a reliable source.

Permissions, attribution, and removal

If you created or represent material shown on the site, identify the work, the URL, your relationship to it, and the action requested. We take credible attribution, licensing, and removal requests seriously. The site is unofficial and does not claim ownership of the embedded game assets.

Response expectations

We review messages as resources allow and cannot guarantee immediate replies. Sending an email does not create a confidential, legal, employment, or business relationship.
