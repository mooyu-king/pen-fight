How to play · 02

01

Start the browser game

Open the game on the homepage. In Desk Legends, choose Desk Quest, AI Training or a friend room, then select competitive play or Co-op Raid. Use Google sign-in for account-backed quest progress when the edition’s account services are connected, or choose guest play without account sync.

02

Choose your pen and your setting

Desk Legends starts with Classic Red, Classic Blue and Classroom. Before a solo quest or training match, pick an unlocked pen and background. The collection below shows all eight skins, their effects and the three background choices.

03

Press, pull back and read the power

Press your own pen and drag away from the intended shot. Pull down-left to shoot up-right. Start with a short pull, watch the aiming guide and leave room for the rebound. In a friend room, press READY on your turn before aiming.

04

Release and let the pens stop

Release to flick. A central hit tends to push; an off-centre hit can add spin. Wait for motion to finish before the next turn. In Desk Legends, move into CANCEL SHOT before releasing if you want to abandon an aim.

05

Finish a challenge, then choose your next goal

In Desk Quest, win and review the star requirements for your difficulty. Better results unlock collection rewards. In a competitive room, compare the match result and agree to a rematch. In Co-op Raid, clear both enemies before the wave limit and keep an ally on the desk to advance. In AI Training, review the feedback and repeat one skill rather than chasing a score.

Pen Fight rules

Knock opponents off and stay on the desk

A confirmed fall from the 3D desk eliminates a pen. Plan both the hit and your rebound: sending yourself over the edge can end your attempt. Let the board settle before judging the result.

Take the turn that belongs to you

In a friend room, check your seat, wait for your turn and press READY before aiming. You control your own pen; players do not all fire at once. Read the multiplayer room guide for invitations, teams and rematches.

Read the objective for your mode

Solo quests add level-specific star requirements. Free-for-all pits individual pens against each other, while 2v2 uses assigned teams. Co-op Raid asks your group to clear enemy waves within an action limit.

Avoid three common mistakes

Pulling toward the target sends the shot the wrong way. Maximum power can carry your pen off after impact. Shooting before your turn is ready does not create a valid room move. Start with a short pull and a clear route.

How to unlock Pen Fight classroom backgrounds

Backgrounds change the surroundings, not the strength of your shot. Preview and equip them for solo quests and training. The room scene in multiplayer is controlled separately; a personal background does not change everyone else’s room.

Start with Classroom, then clear Clean Strike (quest 3) for Warm Study and Long Slide (quest 6) for Evening Class. Any difficulty counts; you do not need three stars for these scene rewards. Clear preceding quests to open the next level, then visit Collection to preview and equip your unlocked scene.

Desk Guard, Twin Flanks and Crossfire are Raid battle layouts, not additional collectible backgrounds. Raid medals and AI Training results do not unlock these scenes.

Game physics · 03

How Pen Fight turns a mouse drag into a bouncing desk shot

Reverse-direction launch vector

The direction of the pull is opposite to the direction of the shot: drag left to shoot right, down to shoot up, or diagonally away from your target. Pull distance increases power up to a limit. A controlled short shot is often enough when an opponent is already near an edge.

Linear speed and spin

The contact point matters as much as force. A centred collision mainly pushes the other pen; an off-centre hit can turn it and redirect both pieces. Plan the attack and the space you need after contact.

Collisions and obstacles

Desk Legends uses a 3D desk with solid obstacles. Eraser gates, a diagonal ruler and a corner book change the route to the target. Aim around an end or through an open lane instead of repeatedly firing into the same blocked line.

Friction, turns, and the edge rule

Wood gives a different slide from smoother plastic, and wet areas require extra care. In Desk Legends, a confirmed fall off the desk decides elimination. Decorative skins never change the physical rules.

Tactics notebook · 04

Pen Fight strategy: better angles, safer power, and shorter knockout routes

Build consistent shots with these Pen Fight angle and power habits. In a friend room, read your own seat and pen rather than assuming that red is always yours.

Step one: identify the nearest edge

Do not aim at the blue pen’s center automatically. Compare its distance to the top, bottom, left, and right boundaries, then choose the shortest knockout route. If blue is nearest the top edge, your red pen should hit upward—which means pulling the mouse downward because the launch reverses the drag. The goal is not the biggest impact; it is the least force needed to cross the nearest line.

Step two: use center hits to push and side hits to turn

Contact near the blue pen’s center transfers linear motion more directly and is ideal when blue already points toward an edge. Contact near a side or end adds more rotation, which can turn an awkward horizontal target toward the boundary but is harder to predict. Practice center hits first. Use off-center contact when blue has an unfavorable angle or when a straight hit would carry red along the same dangerous route.

Step three: control power instead of always pulling fully

Force increases with drag distance, but more is not always better. Red keeps moving after contact, so an excessive direct hit may eject blue and still rebound red across another boundary. Use a short or medium pull when blue is already near an edge. Reserve longer pulls for large gaps where red also has plenty of open desk behind its expected rebound.

Step four: plan red’s second path

Every shot contains two routes: the attack before contact and the rebound afterward. If red is near the left boundary, avoid a contact angle that sends it farther left. The ideal collision pushes blue along its shortest route out while redirecting red toward the middle. Even without an immediate knockout, finishing near the center reduces the bot’s chance of winning with one short return shot.

Step five: read the edge before the next shot

A confirmed fall from the 3D desk eliminates a pen. A light finishing push is safer than a maximum-power chase when your own pen is near an edge.

A practical training routine

Begin with ten purely horizontal or vertical shots to build reverse-drag muscle memory. Next, use one angle with short, medium, and long pulls to compare distance. Finally, practice hitting blue at the center, one-quarter point, and end. Changing only one variable per attempt teaches speed, rotation, and rebound distance faster than random maximum-power flicks.

Rules & rhythm · 05

Win the desk. Choose your next challenge.

Desk Quest: progress with a purpose

Ten levels move from First Flick, Edge Lesson and Clean Strike to Eraser Gates, Diagonal Deflection, Long Slide, Book Fort, Side Corridor, Wet Routes and Desk Master. Rookie, Standard and Expert have separate star totals. Finishing a quest opens the next; replaying for better stars works toward a new skin.

Rookie rewards School Green and Purple Ink, Standard rewards Orange Pop and Midnight, and Expert rewards Silver and Golden Scholar. Each pair unlocks at 3 and 9 total stars in that difficulty. Backgrounds follow first clears on any difficulty: quest 3, Clean Strike, unlocks Warm Study; quest 6, Long Slide, unlocks Evening Class. Quest 5, Diagonal Deflection, collects Smooth Plastic. Open Collection to preview and equip an unlocked background before a solo match.

Friend rooms: one desk, up to four seats

Use an invitation link or room code to meet friends. Choose free-for-all or 2v2, or switch to Co-op Raid for a shared objective.

AI Training: practice the shot you need

Pick a difficulty, wood or plastic, and an obstacle setup. Repeat a tricky route without chasing quest stars. Fixed keeps the selected opponent difficulty. Optional Adaptive reviews three completed games and adjusts at most one difficulty step for the next game.

Training feedback uses what happened in the match, including missed hits and risky stopping positions. Adaptive changes decisions, not mass, friction or your shot power. Its learning resets when you leave the training session.

Desk Legends guide

Pen Fight Co-op Raid: three waves with friends

Create or join a friend room, choose Co-op Raid and invite at least one other human player. Two to four humans can play; bots fill the remaining seats on your four-pen team. Two enemy pens have their own slots. The host chooses a mission before the team readies up.

Choose your Raid mission

  • Desk Guard: begin facing the enemy pair, follow them to wider positions in wave two and approach a tighter formation in the final wave.
  • Twin Flanks: your team starts around the centre while enemies occupy opposite sides. Check both edges before committing to a push.
  • Crossfire: allies and enemies start across opposing corners. Plan a clear route without knocking a teammate into danger.

Clear waves and face Desk Warden

Each mission has three waves and a 24-action limit per wave, including enemy turns. Knock out both enemies while at least one ally remains. Allied pens return for the next wave; the final pair is Warden Guard and Desk Warden. Boss decisions change the challenge, not pen mass or shot strength.

All allies falling off, or reaching the action limit, ends the attempt. The result shows wave actions, elapsed time and friendly knockouts. Use it to improve your next attempt, then vote to retry with the connected team.

Raid medals and shared challenges

A full clear awards teammates a Raid medal and a win within that room. Raid does not award Desk Quest stars or unlock collection backgrounds. You can also share a timed Raid challenge and match its mission and human seats. Shared targets are unverified, include waiting and reconnect time, and award no extra unlocks.

Try a timed Raid challenge

A shared Raid target specifies a mission and human seats. The room host applies it before the match; the team must match those seats to start. Changing the mode or mission clears the applied target.

The time includes waiting and reconnects, and a shared target is not a verified leaderboard record. It adds a goal for your group, not extra collection rewards.

Keep your progress

Guest play, Google sign-in and cloud saves

Guest play uses temporary progress in the current tab. Choosing Exit clears that guest session. For account-backed progress, use Google sign-in and check the cloud-save status before leaving. Quest stars, unlocked collection items and equipped choices are separate from the medals and wins recorded inside a multiplayer room.

Cloud saving needs a working connection to the account service. If the game reports pending changes or a sync error, keep it open and use its sync controls before switching devices. Do not treat an equipped skin on screen as proof that the upload has finished.

Browser desk · 05

Desktop, mobile, fullscreen, and troubleshooting

Desktop is the recommended setup

Use a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari with JavaScript enabled. A mouse offers the clearest drag-and-release gesture. Keep browser zoom near 100 percent if the pointer and pen appear misaligned, and avoid beginning a drag outside the canvas. Fullscreen can create more visual room, and wait for the game view to resize before aiming again.

Mobile play is experimental

Desk Legends needs further phone and tablet testing. This site scales the canvas visually so it fits narrower screens, but visual scaling cannot guarantee that every device maps touch coordinates perfectly. Landscape orientation gives the arena more width. If a finger press lands away from the visible pen, return to normal browser zoom or use a desktop computer rather than repeatedly fighting the offset.

If the game does not start

Select Enter the 3D desk in the player. If loading stalls, reload once and check whether a privacy extension blocked game scripts or assets. Keep the game tab open while it loads. If the problem continues, send your browser version and the error shown to our support contact.

If the skin preview is not rotating

Check the rotation button and device motion preferences. Follow our animation settings guide for Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad and Android, or keep reduced motion and drag the preview manually.

If the canvas is blank

Wait a moment, then reload once. A persistent blank area can point to blocked JavaScript, a failed asset request, or an unsupported browser. The 3D classroom and game assets can take a moment to load. Opening the player in fullscreen will not repair a missing script. Update the browser, temporarily disable aggressive script blocking for penfight.org, and try again.

If dragging does nothing

Click directly on your own pen, hold, move, and release. In a Desk Legends room, check your seat and wait for your turn. Also confirm that no game-over or menu overlay is covering the canvas. Resume from the menu or choose Restart to restore an active round.

Privacy and local hosting

The core game is served from this same domain rather than framed from a third-party deployment. That reduces dependency on an external live copy, but the website host and lightweight analytics service may still receive standard technical information. Read the Privacy Policy for details. The Desk Legends edition also supports account services for Google sign-in and cloud quest progress. Guest play does not sync to an account; room medals remain separate from collection saves.
