About PenFight.org

About Us

The school-desk game, online

PenFight.org is home to Pen Fight: Desk Legends, our browser game inspired by the classroom pastime of flicking pens across a desk. The goal is familiar: knock an opponent off while keeping your own pen on the table.

What we are building

Desk Legends brings that idea into a 3D classroom, with solo Desk Quest challenges, collectible pen skins and backgrounds, AI practice and rooms for friends. Players can compete in free-for-all or 2v2, or team up in Co-op Raid. Desktop is the recommended way to play during the beta; mobile controls still need further testing.

Our guides

Our controls, tactics and unlock guides are based on the game’s implementation. We aim to explain what each mode does, how rewards work and which limitations remain. Cosmetic skins do not grant extra shot power. When we update a feature, its guide should reflect the same rules.

Original work and third-party credits

Desk Legends original code, interface and written content are maintained by the PenFight.org contributors.
