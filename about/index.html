About Us

What penfight.org is

penfight.org is an independent, unofficial browser portal built around a small Pen Fight web prototype. We make the game easy to launch, explain its drag-and-release controls, document practical browser limitations, and publish original guidance for players who remember pen fighting as a school-desk pastime.

Our purpose

The site is designed for casual visitors looking for a quick free browser game. The surrounding interface, written guide, FAQ, compatibility notes, and site code are maintained independently. The playable game package and its original artwork come from its public GitHub repository and remain associated with their original contributors.

Unofficial status and ownership

We are not the official developer, publisher, or rights holder of the embedded prototype, and no endorsement is implied. Game names, code, images, and other creator-owned materials belong to their respective owners. The upstream repository did not display a clear license file when this site was prepared; rights holders may contact us about attribution, permission, correction, or removal.

Editorial approach

Our guides are written for this website rather than copied from repository documentation. We distinguish confirmed game behavior from recommendations, describe mobile input as experimental, and avoid promising features such as multiplayer or persistent saves that the current build does not provide.
